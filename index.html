Marc Performance OS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Marc Performance OS ("the Application") is a personal fitness, football performance, recovery and training-management tool created and operated by Marc Artigues ("we", "us" or "the developer").

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application may access, how that information is used, where it may be processed, and how access can be withdrawn.

1. Information we may access

WHOOP data

With the user's explicit authorization through WHOOP OAuth, the Application may access data made available through the WHOOP Developer API, including:

  • Recovery data, including Recovery Score, heart-rate variability (HRV) and resting heart rate.
  • Sleep data, including sleep duration, sleep performance and sleep-stage information.
  • Workout data, including workout duration, activity and Strain-related information.
  • Cycle data, including daily Strain and average heart rate.
  • Body measurements, including height, weight and maximum heart rate, where available.

Google Calendar data

If Google Calendar is connected, the Application may access calendar information needed to plan and coordinate training, including event titles, dates, times, durations and relevant event details. The Application may create or update training events that it is authorized to manage. User-created football commitments and other protected events are intended to remain under the user's control.

Training and lifestyle information

The Application may store information voluntarily entered by the user, including workouts, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, RIR/RPE, football sessions, match and trial schedules, nutrition information, recovery information, habits, goals, measurements and training notes.

2. How information is used

Information is used solely to operate and improve the user's personal coaching system, including to:

  • Generate and adapt training sessions.
  • Track training history and progress.
  • Assess recovery and training readiness.
  • Coordinate training with football matches, trials and other calendar commitments.
  • Provide nutrition, recovery and training recommendations.
  • Display dashboards, statistics and historical trends.

WHOOP data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

3. Third-party services

The Application may rely on third-party services to provide its functionality, including WHOOP, Google and Anthropic/Claude. Data may be processed by these providers as necessary to provide the functionality requested by the user and in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The Application does not intentionally disclose WHOOP data to unrelated third parties or sell personal information.

4. Data storage and security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. WHOOP OAuth credentials and tokens must be stored securely and must not be exposed in client-side code or publicly accessible repositories.

Data transmitted between the Application and connected services should use encrypted HTTPS connections where supported.

5. Access and authorization

WHOOP access is granted through WHOOP's OAuth authorization flow. The user may withdraw the Application's access through the relevant WHOOP controls and/or by disabling the integration.

Google Calendar access is controlled through Google's authorization and account controls. The user may revoke access through their Google account permissions.

6. Data retention and deletion

The Application is intended primarily for personal use. Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality and maintain the user's training history, unless the user requests deletion or applicable law requires otherwise.

When a connected integration is disabled, the Application should stop requesting new data from that integration. Previously stored information will be deleted when the user requests deletion, subject to any technical or legal retention requirements.

7. User rights

Depending on the user's location and applicable law, the user may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection to processing of personal information.
